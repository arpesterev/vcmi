# HoMM3 Misfortune integration scope

Adds direct basic/advanced targeted and expert mass Misfortune through the existing native spell chooser and factory controller. The pinned source is sushi-shi/homm3-decomp e12906accf96c2662b1abadbc49ddc111991bf21, ai_tactical.cpp getMisfortuneValue, aiValueOfLuck and valueOfLuckAndMorale.

The scalar implements source float-stored good/bad luck constants promoted to double, clamped negative luck change, resistance before final truncation and source duration. It covers signed initial luck. Native admission requires positive-luck shooting targets whose initial luck is at least the spell reduction. Default VCMI clamps negative luck to zero; zero-luck and cross-zero casts therefore abstain rather than price an unexecuted negative-luck effect. Existing Fortune/Misfortune replacement or cancellation is not supported. Preview must preserve all non-Misfortune mechanical bonuses and unit state, with exactly one canonical negative luck bonus. Unsupported profiles fall back conservatively.

Source enemyCanAttack membership uses the sufficient ranged branch of findEnemyAttacks/addEnemy: an active hostile shooter can shoot at a living mobile friendly. This branch sets the attack bit independently of target ranking and lucky-strike state. Positive-luck melee target commitments remain unsupported, without relaxing global zero-luck exchange certificates. The source killsOnly exclusion and planning duration are retained. Unlike Sorrow, Misfortune has no source winLikely rejection; known projected-dead targets are suppressed when a matching prefix supplies that state. Missing forecast remains current-state census, not full post-simulation equivalence. Timing attack facts are initialized for multi-stack targeted decisions.

This completes the direct positive/negative luck spell pair only within these domains, not all luck effects, original global spell ranking, future lucky-turn simulation or full HoMM3 AI parity. Initial native tests exposed the default negative-luck clamp; revised positive-luck domain validation pending.
